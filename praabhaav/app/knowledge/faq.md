## Payments
- Creators are paid by UPI to the UPI ID they submitted with their reel.
- Payment is sent after the team verifies the reel. Status flow:
  submitted (received) → approved (verified, in the payment queue) → scheduled → paid.
- The target is to pay within 24–48 hours of approval.
- Sometimes payments take longer because of daily UPI transfer limits on our side.
  When this happens payments go out in order of submission, oldest first.
- "Paid" means the money was sent. If a creator sees "paid" but has not received it,
  they should check the UPI app for the exact UPI ID they submitted; if it still
  isn't there, the team will check it with the transaction reference.

## Changing details
- A UPI ID or amount can only be changed by the Praabhaav team, never by the bot.
  Requests to change them are always passed to the team.

## Reels
- A reel must stay live and use the campaign's song as its audio.
- If a reel is marked "issue", the note on the status page says what to fix.
  After fixing it, the creator should reply on WhatsApp so the team can re-check.

## Contact
- Creators and clients can reach the team on WhatsApp. Escalated queries are
  answered by the core team, usually within one working day.
