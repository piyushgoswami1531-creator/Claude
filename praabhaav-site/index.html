FAQ

Questions people ask us

How much does a campaign cost?

It depends on your budget and goals. Creators are priced per reel, and we build the creator list to fit what you want to spend. Message us with your song and budget for a plan.

What kind of songs do you promote?

Any song that's available as Instagram audio: independent releases, label tracks, regional and Bollywood-style music. We match creators whose audience suits the sound.

How do I see my results?

You get a private report link for your campaign. It shows total views, a views-over-time chart, likes, reach and every live reel ranked by views. It updates every day.

I'm a creator. How and when do I get paid?

Over UPI. After you submit your reel, it's checked automatically, and your dashboard shows the expected payment date. Payments go out oldest-first, so nobody gets skipped.

Why might my payment take a day or two?

UPI has daily sending limits, so large campaigns are paid in daily batches. That's why we show you the exact expected date instead of leaving you guessing.

Do I need a big following to be a creator with you?

No. We work with accounts of many sizes; what matters is that your audience fits the campaign. Send us your profile and we'll let you know.
